10 Things After 10 Months
That Still Make Riya Awesome

  1. She doesn't just enter hackathons — she wins them. IEEE first place, TechSprint champion. She collects trophies the way other people collect excuses not to try.
  2. She can walk up to a projector screen, open her mouth in front of a crowd, and make every single person lean forward. That kind of presence isn't taught — it's hers.
  3. She is the Actual, Official, Trophy-Holding Musical Chair Champion of the world. There is photographic evidence. The plaque says so.
  4. She turns every mirror she passes into a whole moment. Leopard print, grey tank, red ruffles — she makes every single thing look like it was made for her.
  5. She climbs on shoulders at concerts and screams every word to the song and it is, genuinely, the most alive thing I have ever seen.
  6. She made flowers in someone's hair look like a poem once. Just by being the one to put them there.
  7. She makes video calls feel like the best part of any day — even when she's making that face into the camera from two centimetres away.
  8. She could turn two shadows on the pavement into the most beautiful photograph in the world. Jan 23, 15:38. I will never forget.
  9. She shows up to present a whole AI system at a hackathon, carries the room entirely, and then sits down like it was nothing. It is never nothing.
  10. She made 10 months feel like a beginning. Not a milestone — a beginning.

What I Keep Trying To Say

There is a version of me from before all of this — before your laugh and your glasses and your completely unhinged competitive streak — and I genuinely have no idea what that version was doing with his time. You walked in, and quietly, without announcement, rearranged the whole thing. Not loudly. Not all at once. The way a song gets inside you before you realise you're already humming it.

I fell in love with how hard you work. How you hold your arguments like they're something sacred and worth protecting. How you show up for the people you love — fully, without hesitation — with a kind of loyalty that some people spend their whole lives looking for.

I fell in love with the way you feel things. A song becomes a whole memory. A shadow on the ground becomes the most romantic photograph. You make the most ordinary Tuesday feel like something worth keeping.

Ten months ago I didn't know a song could carry something this large. That I'd be here, still finding new words for it, still not quite getting there. Here are ten more attempts. There will be ten more after those. And ten more after those. Because you, Riya — you are the kind of person worth running out of words over.
